# Northline EV — validation plan

## Goal
Prove that a legally viable electric/new-energy motorhome can be sourced for Germany and that real DACH buyers will express high-intent demand before meaningful inventory capital is committed.

## Stage gates
1. **Supplier evidence:** obtain written technical specs, FOB price, demo availability, MOQ, battery/charging details, warranty and EU approval evidence.
2. **Homologation gate:** no vehicle is presented as orderable until its approval path is reviewed with a German technical service/approval expert. EU Regulation 2018/858 is the core framework; §21 StVZO is a possible German individual-vehicle route where no approved type exists.
3. **Unit economics:** build landed-cost scenarios including freight, customs/duties where applicable, adaptation, homologation/testing, warranty reserve, German VAT, payment/FX risk and dealer margin.
4. **Demand:** collect qualified early-access leads. Target: 10 serious interviews, 3 written high-intent expressions, then test a refundable reservation only after legal/commercial terms are ready.
5. **Demo:** import one demonstrator only after Gates 1–4 support the decision.

## Demand questions
Capture more than email: country/postcode, target format, seats/beds, licence/3.5t constraint, desired real-world range, budget band, purchase horizon, home charging, must-have features, willingness to visit a Munich demo, and willingness to place a refundable reservation if homologation/spec/price are confirmed.

## Success criteria
Do not optimize for traffic. Optimize for qualified buyer conversations, homologation certainty, and credible gross margin.
