# Buyer discovery — first 10 interviews

## Hypothesis
A DACH buyer exists who wants a genuinely usable electric motorhome but is underserved by today's European range, payload and price trade-offs.

## Interview — 15 minutes
Do not pitch first.

1. What do you drive/camp with today?
2. How many people sleep and travel in it?
3. Typical trip: weekend, Alps, Scandinavia, southern Europe?
4. Longest driving day?
5. Is <=3.5t licence compatibility mandatory?
6. Minimum real motorway range before you would consider electric?
7. Home charging available?
8. Which matters most: range, payload, bathroom, fixed bed, AWD, price?
9. What price would feel surprisingly cheap / reasonable / expensive / impossible?
10. Would you travel to Munich to test a validated demonstrator?
11. If exact specification, German registration and final price were confirmed, would you consider a refundable reservation? At what amount?

## Qualification
High-intent = purchase horizon <=18 months + budget plausible + concrete use case + willingness to see demo or reserve conditionally.

## Initial target
10 interviews -> 3 high-intent prospects -> only then test a reservation mechanism.
