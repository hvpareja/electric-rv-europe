# Import economics — critical tariff risk

## Finding (5 Oct 2026)
Do **not** model a Chinese BEV motorhome with a generic 10% vehicle duty assumption until the exact customs classification is established.

EU Implementing Regulation 2024/2754 imposes definitive countervailing duties on Chinese new BEVs principally designed for transport of <=9 persons, including vehicles with an ICE range extender, when falling under ex CN 8703 80 10 / TARIC 8703 80 10 10.

Published definitive rates include 17.0% BYD, 18.8% Geely, 35.3% SAIC, 20.7% other cooperating companies and 35.3% other non-cooperating companies (subject to the exact current regulation/company code).

## Northline action
For every candidate model obtain:
1. Exact manufacturer/exporter legal entity.
2. Proposed CN/TARIC classification used for EU import.
3. Evidence of any prior EU customs clearance for the exact completed motorhome.
4. Whether the producer appears under a company-specific additional TARIC code.
5. Written customs-broker opinion before pricing.
6. Consider an EU Binding Tariff Information (BTI) application for legal certainty. A BTI is generally valid for 3 years and binds EU customs administrations and the holder.

## Scenario model
Until classification is confirmed, run at least:
- Scenario A: standard third-country duty only.
- Scenario B: standard duty + 20.7% countervailing duty.
- Scenario C: standard duty + 35.3% countervailing duty.

Never advertise an all-in retail price from Scenario A alone.

## VAT
German import VAT is normally 19%; its base includes customs value plus customs duty and relevant charges. For a VAT-registered business, treat recoverability/cash-flow separately from economic cost and confirm with tax adviser.
